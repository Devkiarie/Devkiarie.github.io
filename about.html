Ian Kiarie

Engineering Student | Full-Stack Developer | AI/ML Builder

I'm a hands-on full-stack developer who enjoys turning ideas into production-ready systems. I build modern web UIs, backend APIs, and data-driven features with an eye for reliability, performance, and clean architecture.

Over time I've grown into a more well-rounded engineer — comfortable across Angular/React on the frontend, Django/Python and TypeScript backends, and practical infrastructure like Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Supabase. I also build Android apps in Android Studio, plus ML/AI features and LLM-powered experiences using tools like Amazon Bedrock and Ollama.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me dancing—it's my ultimate stress reliever and creative outlet. From Afrobeat to trap, movement is my second language. I believe rhythm in dance mirrors rhythm in code—both require timing, structure, and a touch of personal flair.
