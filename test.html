Ian Kiarie

Engineering Student | Full-Stack Developer | Creative Designer

I'm a passionate technologist with a creative edge, constantly evolving through learning and hands-on experience. My journey blends analytical engineering with artistic expression—whether I'm architecting robust systems, designing intuitive interfaces, or sketching concepts in my notebook.

What drives me? The thrill of solving complex problems and the joy of creating elegant solutions. I thrive on feedback and continuous improvement, pushing myself to grow both technically and personally every single day.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me dancing—it's my ultimate stress reliever and creative outlet. From Afrobeat to Kizomba, movement is my second language. I believe rhythm in dance mirrors rhythm in code—both require timing, structure, and a touch of personal flair.

My academic journey has given me a strong theoretical foundation, while my self-driven projects have honed my practical skills in software development and system design.
